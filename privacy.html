Overview

UnlockMxPro ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our services or connect your social accounts to our platform.

Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide our services:

  • Facebook Page Data — page name, profile picture, access tokens, and post engagement metrics, collected solely to schedule and publish social media posts on behalf of page administrators who explicitly grant permission.
  • Contact Information — name, email address, and phone number provided voluntarily when you contact us.
  • Usage Data — basic analytics such as pages visited and features used, to improve our platform.

How We Use Your Data

  • To schedule and publish social media posts on connected Facebook pages.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data or Facebook page data with third parties for marketing or any other purposes. Data is only shared with third-party services strictly necessary to operate our platform (e.g., hosting providers), under confidentiality obligations.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls, and regular security audits to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Revoke Facebook permissions at any time via your Facebook account settings.
  • Opt out of any non-essential data processing.
